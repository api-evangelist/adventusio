Adventus Professional Services (APS) is Adventus.io’s admissions and compliance servicing arm, now available to institutions. APS helps institutions efficiently manage increased volume of varying-quality applications. London Metropolitan University has seen positive results since adopting APS, supporting its student recruitment, marketing, admissions, and compliance services in South Asia and Sub-Saharan Africa.
